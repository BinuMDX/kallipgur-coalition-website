Frequently Asked
Questions.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Yes. Kallipgur Coalition Aboriginal Corporation is a registered charity and holds Deductible Gift Recipient (DGR) status. All donations over $2 are tax-deductible in Australia.

Can I set up a regular monthly donation?

Absolutely. Regular giving provides us with a predictable income stream, allowing us to plan long-term community programs. You can choose 'Monthly' when making your donation above.

Can my business make a corporate donation?

Yes, we welcome partnerships with businesses that share our values. If you are interested in a corporate partnership or sponsorship, please contact us directly via our Contact page.

Where exactly does my money go?

Funds are directed to areas of greatest need as determined by our community-elected Board. This typically includes youth education, Elders support, cultural preservation, and emergency community relief.
