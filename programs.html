Our community-controlled programs are designed by our people, for our people. We focus on holistic wellbeing, cultural strength, and creating sustainable pathways for the future.

01
Cultural Heritage

Language & Cultural Revival

Our cultural heritage program is the cornerstone of Kallipgur Coalition. Led by Elders and cultural knowledge holders, this initiative works to document, teach, and celebrate our traditional languages and practices.

  • Weekly language immersion classes for all ages
  • Digital archiving of oral histories and songs
  • On-Country cultural camps for young men and women
  • Traditional land management and ecological knowledge workshops
02
Health & Wellbeing

Holistic Community Health

We provide culturally safe, community-controlled health services that address physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual wellbeing. We treat the whole person in the context of their family and community.

  • Primary healthcare clinic staffed by Aboriginal health workers
  • Social and emotional wellbeing counseling
  • Maternal health and early childhood support
  • Chronic disease management and nutrition education
03
Youth & Future

Youth Empowerment Hub

Investing in our young people is investing in our future. The Youth Empowerment Hub provides a safe, culturally affirming space for Aboriginal youth to learn, create, and develop leadership skills.

  • After-school tutoring and homework support
  • Youth leadership and governance training
  • Creative arts, music, and digital media workshops
  • Mentorship programs pairing youth with Elders and professionals
04
Economic Independence

Economic Development & Employment

We actively work to build economic independence for our people by fostering Aboriginal enterprise, providing targeted training, and advocating for meaningful employment opportunities.

  • Small business incubation for Aboriginal entrepreneurs
  • Vocational training and certification partnerships
  • Job placement and ongoing workplace mentoring
  • Financial literacy and wealth-building workshops

Access our services.

If you or a family member would like to participate in any of our programs, or if you need assistance, please reach out to our community team.
